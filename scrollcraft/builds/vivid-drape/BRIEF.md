## The eight topics

1. **Vibe [authored]:** a fitting room with good light, quietly confident, tactile.
   References: the colour-analysis drape session itself (grey cape, fabric swatches
   laid under the chin); a printed fashion monthly's feature well; a tailor's swatch book
   with pinked edges and paper tags.
2. **Journey [authored]:** a title page. The wardrobe problem they recognise. A silent
   turn. The drape, where colour visibly changes a face. What Rathi does. What clients
   said. Who Rathi is. A close that ends in the visitor's colour, with one line to act on.
3. **Energy [authored]:** calm and paper-quiet at the top; a near-empty intertitle as
   held breath; the single loud moment is the drape on a dark stage; then warm and
   steady to a small, resolved close.
4. **Feeling + the one moment [authored]:** see the curve and peak below.
5. **The thing no other site does [authored]:** it performs the actual service. Scroll
   lays fabric under a face, one season at a time, and the visitor watches the face
   change. Then the visitor picks the colour they kept looking at, and the whole page
   (accent, the testimonial ground, the closing plate, the booking email) becomes theirs.
6. **Range [user → Editorial]:** printed feature, folio in the margin, hard cuts between
   chapter grounds, serif display for fashion-editorial lineage (Bodoni Moda: didone,
   the face fashion magazines are set in) with Geist for text.
7. **One world or scenes [authored]:** distinct scenes. A drape session is a sequence of
   separate trials, not a journey through a place.
8. **Assets [user → free stock]:** Unsplash, no spend, no video. Fabric swatches on the
   title page are built in HTML/CSS (honest objects, not images).
